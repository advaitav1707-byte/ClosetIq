# ClosetIQ

## Product Vision

ClosetIQ is an AI-powered personal fashion intelligence platform that helps users style what they already own, understand their personal style, and make smarter fashion decisions.

Unlike a traditional digital wardrobe, ClosetIQ considers the complete fashion ecosystem, including clothing, jewelry, footwear, and accessories.

---

## The Problem

People often own many fashion items but still struggle with questions such as:

- What should I wear today?
- How can I style the clothes I already own?
- Which jewelry or accessories would elevate an outfit?
- What footwear works best with a particular look?
- Do I actually need to buy this new item?
- Which items in my wardrobe are being underused?
- What does my personal style actually look like?

ClosetIQ aims to solve these problems through personalized fashion intelligence.

---

## The Three Core Pillars

### 1. Style Me

ClosetIQ generates personalized outfit combinations using items already present in the user's collection.

It considers:

- Clothing
- Jewelry
- Footwear
- Accessories
- Occasion
- Weather
- Personal style
- Color preferences

---

### 2. Shop Smarter

ClosetIQ helps users make more informed fashion purchases.

Before purchasing an item, users can evaluate:

- Wardrobe compatibility
- Similar items already owned
- Potential outfit combinations
- Expected usage
- Cost-per-wear
- Personal style compatibility

The goal is to help users purchase intentionally rather than impulsively.

---

### 3. Know My Style

ClosetIQ learns from the user's wardrobe and outfit behaviour to identify patterns in their personal style.

It can analyse:

- Frequently worn colors
- Preferred categories
- Preferred brands
- Frequently worn items
- Underused items
- Favourite outfit combinations
- Preferred styles and aesthetics
- Shopping behaviour

---

## Signature Feature: Elevate My Look

ClosetIQ can improve an existing outfit without requiring the user to completely change it.

For example, it may recommend:

- Adding jewelry
- Changing footwear
- Adding a bag
- Layering accessories
- Adjusting color combinations
- Making small styling changes

The objective is to demonstrate how styling can transform an existing outfit.

## Core Philosophy

### Wear More. Buy Smarter. Style Better.

ClosetIQ aims to help users get more value from the fashion items they already own while making better purchasing and styling decisions.

---

## Long-Term Vision

ClosetIQ aims to evolve into a personal fashion intelligence platform that understands a user's wardrobe, personal style, fashion behaviour, and purchasing decisions.

The long-term goal is to combine artificial intelligence, fashion technology, personalization, and responsible consumption into a single fashion experience.
