# Product Vision

## Project Name
ClosetIQ

## Tagline
Wear More. Buy Smarter.

## Vision Statement

ClosetIQ is an AI-powered digital wardrobe platform designed to help users organize their clothing, discover outfit combinations, and make smarter fashion decisions while promoting mindful fashion consumption.

## Problem Statement

Many people own a large number of clothes but still struggle with deciding what to wear. This often leads to:

- Buying duplicate clothing items
- Wasting money on unnecessary purchases
- Underutilizing existing wardrobes
- Decision fatigue before outings

## Solution

ClosetIQ provides users with a digital wardrobe where they can:

- Organize clothing
- Create outfit combinations
- Track cost per wear
- Receive personalized outfit suggestions
- Promote mindful shopping habits

## Target Users

- College students
- Young professionals
- Fashion enthusiasts
- Anyone interested in organizing their wardrobe

## Long-Term Goal

To become a smart personal fashion assistant that combines AI, technology, and sustainability to transform the way people interact with their wardrobes.
