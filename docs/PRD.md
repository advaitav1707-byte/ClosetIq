# ClosetIQ — Product Requirements Document

## 1. Product Overview

### Product Name

ClosetIQ

### Product Category

AI-powered Personal Fashion Intelligence

### Product Philosophy

> Wear More. Style Better. Buy Smarter.

### Product Vision

ClosetIQ aims to help users make better fashion decisions by understanding their wardrobe, personal style, preferences, proportions, lifestyle and shopping behaviour.

Rather than functioning only as a digital wardrobe or outfit generator, ClosetIQ connects wardrobe management, styling, complete-look coordination, personal style discovery and purchase intelligence into one personalized experience.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

People often own a large number of fashion items but still struggle with deciding what to wear.

Common problems include:

- Difficulty creating outfits from existing clothes
- Feeling like they have "nothing to wear" despite having a full wardrobe
- Difficulty coordinating clothing with jewelry, footwear and accessories
- Uncertainty about whether an outfit suits their personal preferences and proportions
- Repeatedly purchasing items similar to things they already own
- Buying pieces that do not integrate well with their existing wardrobe
- Difficulty understanding their own evolving personal style
- Generic fashion recommendations that may not reflect individual preferences
- The effort required to manually maintain a digital wardrobe

Existing fashion applications address many of these problems individually, but ClosetIQ aims to connect them into one integrated fashion decision experience.

---

# 3. Target Users

## Primary Target User

Young fashion-conscious users who:

- Own multiple clothing and fashion items
- Frequently think about styling and outfits
- Use social media for fashion inspiration
- Shop both online and offline
- Want personalized fashion recommendations
- Are interested in understanding their personal style
- Want to make better use of their existing wardrobe

## Secondary Target Users

- College students
- Young professionals
- Fashion enthusiasts
- Users interested in sustainable fashion
- Users who frequently shop for clothing and accessories
- Users experimenting with their personal style

---

# 4. Market Gap

Existing fashion-tech platforms provide solutions for individual aspects of wardrobe management, styling, shopping and personalization.

However, users still face the challenge of making context-aware fashion decisions based on:

- Their existing wardrobe
- Personal style
- Fit and proportions
- Occasion
- Weather
- Lifestyle
- Previous fashion behaviour
- Potential wardrobe compatibility

ClosetIQ aims to bridge this gap by creating an integrated personal fashion intelligence platform.

The platform connects wardrobe data, complete-look styling, fit and proportion preferences, personal style evolution and purchase decisions into one personalized experience.

---

# 5. Unique Selling Proposition

ClosetIQ is an AI-powered personal fashion intelligence platform that helps users make better fashion decisions — from styling what they already own and elevating complete looks to understanding what suits them and evaluating what is worth buying.

ClosetIQ focuses on the complete fashion decision rather than only generating outfits.

---

# 6. Core Product Pillars

## 6.1 Style Me

### User Question

> What should I wear?

ClosetIQ generates outfit combinations using items already present in the user's wardrobe.

Recommendations may consider:

- Occasion
- Weather
- Personal style
- Colour preferences
- Fit preferences
- Previously liked outfits
- Wardrobe availability
- Footwear
- Jewelry
- Accessories

---

## 6.2 Elevate My Look

### User Question

> How can I make this outfit better?

Users can provide an existing outfit and receive suggestions to improve or complete the look.

Recommendations may include:

- Jewelry
- Footwear
- Bags
- Belts
- Sunglasses
- Layering
- Colour coordination
- Accessories

The objective is to enhance an existing outfit rather than always recommending a completely new outfit.

---

## 6.3 Shop Smarter

### User Question

> Should I buy this?

Users can evaluate a potential purchase before buying it.

ClosetIQ can consider:

- Similar items already owned
- Compatibility with the existing wardrobe
- Number of potential outfits
- Personal style compatibility
- Fit and proportion preferences
- Potential usage
- Cost-per-wear
- Colour compatibility
- Existing alternatives

The feature is designed to support more informed purchasing decisions.

---

## 6.4 Know My Style

### User Question

> What does my wardrobe say about my personal style?

ClosetIQ analyzes patterns in the user's wardrobe and interactions.

It can identify:

- Frequently worn colours
- Preferred clothing categories
- Preferred silhouettes
- Frequently used accessories
- Preferred jewelry styles
- Preferred footwear
- Frequently worn brands
- Most-used items
- Least-used items
- Outfit preferences
- Shopping patterns

ClosetIQ should allow this style profile to evolve as the user interacts with the platform.

---

## 6.5 Fit & Proportion Intelligence

### User Question

> Is this style likely to work with my preferred fit and proportions?

ClosetIQ may consider user-provided information about:

- Body proportions
- Preferred fits
- Height
- Silhouette preferences
- Areas the user prefers to emphasize or balance

The system should provide styling guidance rather than rigid rules about what a user can or cannot wear.

For example:

> "This silhouette may create the proportions you're looking for."

Instead of:

> "This outfit does not suit your body type."

This feature should respect user choice and avoid restrictive or judgmental recommendations.

# 8. User Journey

## Step 1 — Create Profile

The user provides basic information and fashion preferences.

Possible information includes:

- Preferred aesthetic
- Favourite colours
- Preferred fits
- Basic proportions
- Lifestyle
- Common occasions

---

## Step 2 — Build Wardrobe

The user adds fashion items to their digital wardrobe.

Each item may contain:

- Category
- Subcategory
- Colour
- Pattern
- Brand
- Material
- Fit
- Occasion
- Season
- Image

---

## Step 3 — Discover Style

ClosetIQ analyzes the user's wardrobe and preferences to create an initial style profile.

---

## Step 4 — Generate Looks

The user can request an outfit based on:

- Occasion
- Weather
- Mood
- Personal style
- Selected wardrobe item

ClosetIQ generates a complete look.

---

## Step 5 — Elevate the Look

The user can ask ClosetIQ to improve the outfit.

The system can recommend:

- Jewelry
- Footwear
- Accessories
- Layering
- Colour adjustments

---

## Step 6 — Shop Smarter

The user can upload or describe a potential purchase.

ClosetIQ evaluates:

- Personal compatibility
- Wardrobe compatibility
- Fit and proportion preferences
- Potential outfit combinations
- Similar existing items
- Purchase value

---

## Step 7 — Learn and Adapt

The system learns from user interactions.

Examples:

- Outfit liked
- Outfit disliked
- Item frequently worn
- Item rarely worn
- Purchase made
- Recommendation rejected

This information improves future personalization.

# 11. Functional Requirements

## FR1 — User Profile

The system shall allow users to create and update a fashion profile.

## FR2 — Wardrobe Management

The system shall allow users to add, edit and remove fashion items.

## FR3 — Fashion Categories

The system shall support clothing, jewelry, footwear and accessories.

## FR4 — Outfit Generation

The system shall generate outfit combinations using available wardrobe items.

## FR5 — Outfit Enhancement

The system shall recommend accessories, jewelry and footwear to enhance selected outfits.

## FR6 — Purchase Evaluation

The system shall allow users to evaluate potential purchases against their existing wardrobe.

## FR7 — Style Analysis

The system shall provide basic insights into the user's fashion preferences.

## FR8 — Personalization

The system shall use user preferences and interaction history to improve recommendations.

## FR9 — Feedback

The system shall allow users to provide feedback on recommendations.

---

# 12. Non-Functional Requirements

## Performance

The system should provide recommendations within a reasonable response time.

## Usability

The interface should be simple and intuitive for users with limited technical knowledge.

## Privacy

Personal information and user-uploaded fashion images should be handled securely.

## Scalability

The system should be designed so that additional fashion categories and recommendation capabilities can be added later.

## Accessibility

The interface should aim to provide a clear and accessible user experience.

---

# 13. Success Metrics

ClosetIQ can initially measure:

- Number of wardrobes created
- Number of fashion items added
- Number of outfits generated
- Outfit recommendation acceptance rate
- Outfit recommendation rejection rate
- Number of repeat users
- Number of purchase evaluations
- Number of items reused in generated outfits
- User satisfaction with recommendations

---

# 14. Product Principles

### 1. Personalization Over Generic Advice

Recommendations should become increasingly personalized as ClosetIQ learns from the user.

### 2. Complete Looks Over Individual Items

ClosetIQ should consider clothing, jewelry, footwear and accessories together.

### 3. Guidance Over Restriction

Body and proportion insights should guide users without imposing rigid fashion rules.

### 4. Existing Wardrobe First

The platform should prioritize helping users make better use of what they already own.

### 5. Explainable Recommendations

Where possible, ClosetIQ should explain why a recommendation was made.

### 6. Minimum Effort, Maximum Intelligence

Users should not feel like maintaining their digital wardrobe is a chore.

---

# 15. Long-Term Vision

ClosetIQ aims to evolve from a digital wardrobe application into a personal fashion intelligence system.

The long-term vision is to create an intelligent fashion companion that understands:

- What the user owns
- What the user likes
- What the user wears
- What suits their preferred proportions
- What occasions they dress for
- How their style evolves
- What they are considering purchasing

The ultimate goal is to help users make better fashion decisions throughout their everyday fashion journey.

---

# 16. Core Statement

> ClosetIQ isn't designed to tell people what they should wear.

> It is designed to help people make better fashion decisions.
